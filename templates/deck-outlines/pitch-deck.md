# Pitch deck outline template

Copy to `outputs/pitch/outline.md` and fill in **insight titles**.

## Cover
**Title:** [Company] — [one-line promise]

## Problem
**Title:** [Target customer] loses [cost/time] because [pain]

## Solution
**Title:** We [verb] so customers [measurable outcome]

## Why now
**Title:** [Inflection] makes this inevitable in [timeframe]

## Product
**Title:** [Product name] delivers [core capability] in one flow
**Visual:** screenshot / diagram

## Market
**Title:** $[SAM] addressable market growing [X]% annually
**Visual:** TAM/SAM/SOM

## Traction
**Title:** [Key metric] proves demand — [specific number]
**Visual:** growth chart

## Business model
**Title:** We earn [$/unit] through [model]

## Competition
**Title:** We win on [wedge] vs [alternatives]
**Visual:** 2×2 or landscape

## GTM
**Title:** Beachhead: [segment] via [motion]

## Team
**Title:** [Founder background] + [domain] expertise

## Ask
**Title:** Raising $[X] to [3 use-of-funds bullets]

## Contact
**Title:** [Name] · [email] · [url]
